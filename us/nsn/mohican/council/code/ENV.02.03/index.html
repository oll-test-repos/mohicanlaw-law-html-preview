Stockbridge-Munsee Tribal Code

Section ENV.02.03 — Committee Mission Statement

The Committee was established by the Tribal Council to aid in the protection and conservation of the forest resources of the Tribe. The purpose of the Committee is to act as an advocate for and make recommendations to the Tribal Council on all forestry-related matters.

(April 1, 2025, 028-25.)
